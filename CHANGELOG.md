# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- `FloodResult.road_detail`: major vs local roads flooded, bridges flooded, flooded road length (km).

## 0.2.0
- `FloodResult.infrastructure`: flooded counts per infrastructure category (incl. six Islamabad-only categories).

## 0.1.0
- Client for /run, instant flood, coastal (static and dynamic), GLOF, IDF, observed-flood scoring, tehsil/basin search.
- `ensure_server()` to start a local SAILAB on demand.
- `SailabFloodSimulator`, an adapter for SimCity's Simulator contract.
