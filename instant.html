What this is

A screening estimate computed in about a second from the terrain alone: water height above the nearest drainage (HAND) combined with a channel rating curve. It shows where a given flow could reach, not how the flood unfolds over time. It ignores buildings, rain and dams.

For depth over time, velocity, buildings and roads at risk, loss in PKR, coastal, GLOF and protection scenarios, use the full physical model.
